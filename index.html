A kitchen that starts before sunrise

Rosette Bakehouse began in a rented kitchen with one oven and a stand mixer that had seen better days. Eleven years on, we still measure flour by hand, still crack every egg ourselves, and still close the order book once the day's batch is full — because a cake made for fifty people tastes different from one made for five hundred.
