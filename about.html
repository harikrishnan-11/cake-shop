11 years of baking

A kitchen that starts before sunrise

Stackly began in a rented kitchen with one oven and a stand mixer that had seen better days. Priya, our founder, was baking birthday cakes for neighbours between shifts at a hotel kitchen before she took the leap to do it full time. Eleven years on, we still measure flour by hand, still crack every egg ourselves, and still close the order book once the day's batch is full — because a cake made for fifty people tastes different from one made for five hundred.

What we hold onto as we grow

A few things that haven't changed since the first cake left that rented kitchen.

01

Small batches, on purpose

We cap daily orders so every cake gets the same attention as the first one we ever made.

02

Nothing sits frozen

Sponges are baked within 48 hours of collection — never pulled from a freezer to save time.

03

Honest ingredients

Real butter, real cream, real cocoa. We reduce sugar rather than mask it with more frosting.

04

Say no when we should

If a date or design doesn't leave room to do it properly, we'll tell you before you order.

05

Taste before finish

A cake earns its decoration. We build flavour first and let the outside follow.

06

Pack it to last the ride

Every box is sized and boarded to survive the drive home, not just the photo.

How we got here

The short version of eleven years.

2014

A rented kitchen, one oven

Priya started taking birthday cake orders from a shared community kitchen in Model Town, Ludhiana.

2017

First storefront

Stackly opened its doors on Model Town Road, with Arjun joining as pastry chef.

2020

Chandigarh delivery begins

We started chilled delivery routes to Chandigarh and Mohali to meet growing wedding-season demand.

2023

Best Bakery, Ludhiana Food Awards

Recognised locally for the Honey Rosette, still our most-ordered cake to this day.

2025

8,000th cake delivered

Eleven years, one kitchen, and a team that's grown to four — still baking the same way we started.
